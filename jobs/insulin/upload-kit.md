## Description
The scientists who discovered insulin sold the patent for $1 because they believed it belonged to the world. A century later, a 26-year-old restaurant manager named Alec Smith died rationing it. This is the story of how a one-dollar medicine became one of the most expensive in America: three companies, a 13× price climb, the hidden middlemen called PBMs, and the pressure that finally cracked the wall.
