## Description
For 300,000 years, humans had no coins, no banks and no bills. So how did they get anything? The answer you learned in school, barter, almost certainly never happened. From Kalahari gift networks and 75,000-year-old shell beads to Mesopotamian clay IOUs, cows, cowrie shells and the giant stone money of Yap, here's the real story of what came before money, and why your bank balance works a lot like a stone at the bottom of the sea.
